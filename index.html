The 48-Hour Pune Weekend Blueprint

Structured hour-by-hour so you spend less time in traffic and more time chilling with chai.

Day One

Saturday: Old Pune & FC Vibe

~₹280 Total Budget
08:00 AM ₹70

Chai & Bun Maska at Goodluck

Kick off with hot spiced chai and maska-loaded pav before peak morning queues.

10:30 AM ₹25

Shaniwar Wada & Kasba Peth Trail

Explore Bajirao's ramparts with student concession. Stroll by Tambat Ali brass makers.

01:30 PM ₹80

Fiery Misal Lunch at Bedekar

Authentic local fuel with extra sample tarri. Grab a cooling buttermilk.

04:30 PM ₹60

FC Road Book Stalls & Thick Shake

Browse cheap engineering paperbacks, thrift hoodies, and sip cold coffee.

07:00 PM ₹45

Sunset Chill & JM Road Khau Galli

Wind down with friends watching city twilight followed by hot momos or pani puri.

Transport Hack: PMPML Bus Route #11 connects all stops
Day Two

Sunday: Tekdi, Metro & Camp

~₹270 Total Budget
06:30 AM Free

Sunrise Trail at Vetal Tekdi

Climb from MIT side or Law College slope. Enjoy clear views of the western ghat hills.

09:30 AM ₹110

South Indian Treat at Vaishali

Post-hike reward: steaming Mysore Masala Dosa and strong frothy filter coffee.

12:00 PM ₹20

Metro Joyride: Garware to Civil Court

AC comfort across the river interchange. Fast transit away from Deccan traffic.

03:00 PM ₹50

Koregaon Park & Osho Zen Walk

Quiet bamboo paths, leafy bungalows, and serene photo opportunities in Lane 1.

06:00 PM ₹90

Marz-O-Rin Sandwiches & Camp Walk

Classic chicken chutney roll, apple juice, and evening window shopping on MG Road.

Combined Weekend: ₹550 total per person
